06 — FUTURE GOALS

Big ambition.
Grounded plan.

My long-term direction is the US pharmaceutical industry, approached through preparation, evidence and informed decisions—not assumptions.

“Make every next step something I can learn from, measure, and explain.”
01

Finish B.Pharm with strong foundations

Maintain satisfactory academic performance and understand practical concepts. Keep improving in chemistry, pharmaceutics, analysis and core pharmacy subjects.

02

Develop a credible QA/QC profile

Study introductory GMP/GDP and data integrity, practise Excel and technical writing, and collect genuine evidence of completed skills, permitted project work and training.

03

Gain supervised practical exposure

Seek internships with suitable pharmaceutical manufacturers, testing or research laboratories, and quality-related organisations. Maintain a learning log and describe work accurately.

04

Research employers and realistic routes

Research Pfizer, Johnson & Johnson, Eli Lilly, Merck & Co. and AbbVie as aspirational employers—not confirmed opportunities. Compare real vacancies, qualifications, locations, training and eligibility.

05

Understand overseas requirements early

Verify work-authorisation and study options through official US government sources and university international offices. Pharmaceutical industry roles and licensed pharmacist practice have different requirements.

06

Keep a practical backup plan

Consider suitable QA, QC, production, regulatory affairs or pharmacovigilance roles in India, relevant postgraduate study, or related research and clinical pathways. Review cost, eligibility and outcomes before committing.
